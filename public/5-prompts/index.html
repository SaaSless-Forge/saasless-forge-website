Turn It On — About 2 Minutes, Once

  1. Open Claude (the app on your phone, or claude.ai), go to Settings, and find Connectors.
  2. Connect Google Calendar. Sign in with the Google account you use for work and approve access.
  3. Do the same for Gmail. That's it — now Claude can see your schedule and your inbox, but only when you ask.
Needs a paid Claude plan. Your accounts stay yours — Claude only looks when you ask it to, and never sends or books anything without showing you first.
